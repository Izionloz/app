The four SVG icons here (coolant-temperature, oil-temperature, car-battery, gas-station) are from Material Design Icons
(Pictogrammers, https://pictogrammers.com/library/mdi/, github.com/Templarian/MaterialDesign-SVG), Apache License 2.0.
`eva/tools/render_icons.js` draws them to 144 px PNGs (cream on transparent) and `make_assets.py --comfort` bakes those at 36 px.
